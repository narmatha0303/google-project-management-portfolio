# Five-minute project walkthrough

Complete this after your project evidence is ready.

1. **Context — 30 seconds:** What problem were you solving, for whom, and in what setting?
2. **Goal and role — 30 seconds:** What counted as success, and what did you personally own?
3. **Planning — 60 seconds:** Explain scope, stakeholders, schedule, dependencies, and a major risk. Open the supporting documents.
4. **Decisions and execution — 90 seconds:** Describe a difficult decision, communication challenge, or change. Explain your options, action, and tradeoff.
5. **Results — 60 seconds:** Compare results with goals and show evidence. Clearly identify simulated outcomes.
6. **Reflection — 30 seconds:** What did you learn, and what would you improve?

## Prepare evidence-based answers

- How did you prioritize work under constraints?
- How did you resolve stakeholder disagreement?
- What risk did you identify, and how did you respond?
- What changed during delivery, and how did you handle it?
- How did you measure quality and success?

Use Situation, Task, Action, and Result for answers. State your contribution precisely and avoid unsupported metrics. This portfolio supports interview discussion; it does not imply endorsement or a hiring guarantee.
