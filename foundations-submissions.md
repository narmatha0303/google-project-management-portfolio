## Identifying essential project manager skills

**Recorded grade:** 100%  
**Context:** A coursework scenario about planning a corporate retreat.  
**Type:** Analysis of a supplied scenario, not an event personally delivered.

### Decision-making

> The project manager enabled decision-making by setting clear spending limits and approval requirements for expenses. They also scheduled a video meeting with the client to quickly make important decisions about the dinner menu, lighting, and presentation music before the deadline.

### Communication and escalation

> The project manager communicated through regular status update emails and used phone, email, chat, and video meetings to stay aligned with the client. They escalated expenses over $3,000 for approval and worked closely with stakeholders when client-initiated changes needed to be implemented.

### Flexibility

> The project manager remained flexible by adapting to the client’s request to change the retreat’s theme from the past to the future. They coordinated with the graphic design, materials, and signage teams to implement the changes while continuing to meet the project’s goals.

### Organization

> The project manager used strong organizational skills by creating a spreadsheet to track hundreds of tasks and assign responsibilities to team members. They also managed the $100,000 budget, tracked expenses, sent regular status updates, and prioritized important deadlines.

## Review notes

The responses connect concepts to concrete actions: spending controls, client decisions, communication channels, managing a requested change, and using a task tracker. For an interview, distinguish routine updates from escalation and explain why each action helped. Do not claim that the retreat was personally managed or that its budget represents personal project experience.
