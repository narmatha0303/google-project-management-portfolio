## Module 1 — Fundamentals of project initiation

### Reflection: Project initiation and key components

**Type:** Practice assignment · **Duration:** 27 min  
**Grade:** 100% · **Latest:** 100% · **Highest:** 100% · **Result:** Passed · **Answers added:** October 14, 2026

**Scenario:** Develop a high school's digital grading platform. Teachers enter grades; students and parents view them. Budget is capped at $150,000, with one additional school IT team member available. The target is full teacher adoption within nine months. Attendance and lunch-payment systems are outside scope.

#### 1. Have you read the scenario?

I have!

#### 2. What is the project's goal?

The project's goal is to develop a digital grading platform for the local high school within a maximum budget of $150,000.

#### 3. What outlines the project's scope?

The key component outlining the project's scope is that the project should focus only on the digital grading platform and should not impact other digital platforms like attendance or school lunch payments.

#### 4. What is the project deliverable?

The project deliverable is the digital grading platform for the high school.

#### 5. What outlines the project's success criteria?

The key component outlining the project's success criteria is achieving 100% adoption of the digital grading platform within the high school within the next nine months, requiring full teacher buy-in.

#### 6. Who are the project stakeholders?

The project stakeholders include teachers, school administrators, the school IT department, and the district superintendent.

#### 7. What resources are available?

The resources outlined for the project include a maximum budget of $150,000 and the ability to add one member from the school IT department to the team.
